<section class="intro-section site-section reveal-on-scroll">
  <div class="section-kicker">About me</div>
  <div class="intro-layout">
    <h2>Learning the structure behind degraded observations.</h2>
    <div class="intro-copy">
      <p>
        I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Electronic Science,
        <a href="https://www.nudt.edu.cn/">National University of Defense Technology</a>,
        advised by Prof. <a href="https://www.xiajingyuan.com/">Jingyuan Xia</a> and Prof. Yaowen Fu.
        Previously, I received my bachelor's degree from
        <a href="https://www.neu.edu.cn/">Northeastern University</a> in 2021.
      </p>
      <p>
        My research focuses on statistical learning and optimization for image inverse problems,
        including natural image and hyperspectral image super-resolution, low-level vision,
        and radar imaging. I am always open to research discussions and collaboration.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>
